The ten checks

Everything between "send" and "inbox".

A deliverability consultant runs most of these by hand. So do we — with your actual domain and your actual ESP setup, not a generic score.

Plus a sender inventory: every platform that sends as your domain, which one was added or changed last, and when the numbers started moving. Most tools check whether a record exists. They can't tell you that a second platform quietly started sending in March.

01

SPF

Is your authorization record complete, under the ten-lookup limit, and free of conflicting includes?

02

DKIM

Is the selector your ESP handed you actually published, and aligned with the From domain your customers see?

03

DMARC

Policy, alignment, and whether reporting is quietly failing your own subdomains.

04

Sending domain

Verified inside the ESP? Shared IP, dedicated IP, or stuck mid-warmup? And which one is actually sending.

05

List hygiene

How many bot, role, and never-engaged profiles are inflating your audience — and your bill.

06

Complaints & bounces

The two numbers mailbox providers care about, read from your own dashboard.

07

Reputation

Blocklist status and domain reputation across Google, Outlook, and Yahoo.

08

Content & tracking domain

Spam score, image-to-text ratio, and link domains that don't match the domain that sent the mail.

09

Volume pattern

Imports, sudden spikes, and the shape of your last 30 days — the story your ESP won't tell you.

10

Consent & suppression

The setting that silently stops sends: Shopify marketing consent, double opt-in, and unsubscribe sync.
